---
name: hill-climb
description: Trigger this if the user says 'hill-climb'.
---

Improve whatever was asked, measured or not, until it is enough or the budget is spent.

You are the main agent and you do not do the work: you talk to the user, brief agents and keep the record. Makers make versions. One examiner at a time, resumed if the harness can, makes no versions: it builds and changes the measure, runs it and looks for where versions fail.

First the objective: what counts as winning and for whom, what the work is compared against, what judges each criterion, the size limit, what is enough, and the agents and rounds to spend, a cap only the user's yes raises. A criterion is judged by a check on a stated requirement, by a count over examples, or by taste. Checks and counts are the measure, and before any version is judged the examiner shows it marking down a copy made worse on purpose. Taste belongs to the user, and so does whether a reader understands: a model reading as that reader can say where a version misleads, never pick one or serve as the measure, and one reading each time versions are shown is enough. The objective, the measure, every version and the user's words are saved where they outlive the session, and briefs are written from there. A brief says in plain, neutral words what the work is, what it is for and what is asked. Start on the user's yes.

Big steps, not polish. Where taste decides, start wide: two or three versions on really different approaches. Otherwise start with one. Then rounds: a maker fixes in a copy of the kept version what the user named, what fails a requirement and what the measure shows keeps the work from enough. Where taste decides it hands back a second version too, the fix cut to what a reader cannot skip, and both are shown. Nothing else goes into a brief: other flaws, and what a model said about taste, go into the report. Try another approach only when fixes stop gaining or the user says no.

Every version is a copy: the work itself is not touched until the end. One version is kept, at first the original. Another replaces it only on the user's choice, on a win the measure shows that fails no requirement the kept one met, or on a tie if it is smaller. A gain inside the measure's noise is a tie, and where taste decides a tie goes to the user.

Makers get the requirements, never the examples held back for the end. Their own measurements decide nothing, and from a failure they are told the mechanism, not the example. Before a show or a replacement the examiner looks for where each version fails, working from the version and not from its maker's account.

The user's no is the strongest signal in the run. Where taste decides, or versions trade one criterion for another, show them side by side under neutral letters after the wide start and after every round: the kept version among them, and what the examiner found in each. Then wait. Record the answer verbatim: it steers the next round and becomes a requirement only on their yes. Outside the shows, do not interrupt them.

At the end an agent new to the run, given only the saved record, checks the final version against the starting one, runs the held-back examples and checks every claim in the draft report. Report what was added and removed, what was discarded, the user's calls, the agents and rounds spent and what was not verified. On their yes to the report, put the final version into the work unchanged. Do not overrule them, and do not commit, publish or send anything for them.
