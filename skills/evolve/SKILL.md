---
name: evolve
description: Push anything further through adversarial rounds, measured or not, such as code, feature logic, UX, UI or taste. Use when the user says "evolve".
---

Improve whatever was asked, measured or not, as far as the budget allows.

Work like a GAN: those who generate and those who attack stay independent and fight hard, on evidence the other side has checked. Agreement proves nothing, doubt kills nothing, and nobody wins by pleasing the judge. What survives is the list of real problems, and it drives the next step.

Flawless work that has not moved the long-term goal has lost. Reaching the goal moves it forward, in the open. It never shrinks to settle an argument.

Spiral through small steps and huge leaps of faith, never in circles. What was learned outlives every agent. The best version is always in place, and removing counts as much as adding.

Push a number when there is one, but it serves the goal and never replaces it. Never present argument as proof.

Do not interrupt the user, and do not overrule them: their decisions stay in the work. At the end show the taste calls, the open bets and what you would change.
